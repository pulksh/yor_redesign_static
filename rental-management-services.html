A LITTLE MORE CLARITY.

Your questions, answered.

What does the management service include?

The offering includes tenant discovery, onboarding, property operations, invoicing, collections and renewals. The exact responsibilities and fees are set out in your service agreement.

Can you help with property maintenance?

Yes. Property upkeep and service-request coordination are part of the offering. Larger improvements can be discussed with the Projects & Home Services team.

Do you guarantee rent or investment returns?

No guarantee of rental yield, occupancy or future value is made on this website. Commercial terms and service responsibilities are agreed separately.
