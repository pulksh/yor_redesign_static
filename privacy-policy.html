1. Introduction

YourOwnROOM is committed to respecting your privacy and handling personal information responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, disclosed and otherwise processed when you visit our website, contact us, request information, or use our rental management, property-related, project or home services.

In this Policy, “we”, “us” and “our” refer to the YourOwnROOM business operating the relevant website or service. The relevant contracting entity may be YourOwnROOM Private Limited or PropShield Technologies Private Limited, depending on the service and the agreement with you. Please check the applicable service agreement or communication to identify the responsible entity.

2. Scope

This Policy applies to personal information processed in connection with our website, enquiries, customer and property-owner relationships, tenant or occupant interactions, vendor and service provider interactions, and related business communications. Separate notices or contractual terms may apply to particular services and transactions. This Policy does not cover third-party websites or services that we do not control.

3. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect the following categories of information:

· Identity and contact details, such as name, email address, telephone number and correspondence details.

· Enquiry and service information, such as the service requested, preferences, communications, feedback and support history.

· Property and transaction-related information, where relevant to a service, such as property address, ownership or authorisation details, tenancy or occupancy details, lease-related information and service records.

· Business and payment-related information, where required to provide a service, such as invoices, transaction references and payment status. Payment card or banking credentials should be handled by the relevant payment provider and should not be collected by us unless specifically required and securely supported.

· Technical and usage information, such as IP address, browser and device information, approximate location derived from network data, pages viewed, access times, referring pages and diagnostic logs.

· Information you choose to provide in forms, uploaded documents, emails, telephone calls or other communications.

4. How We Collect Information

We may collect information:

· Directly from you when you submit a website form, email us, call us, request a callback, register, or use our services.

· From property owners, tenants, occupants, authorised representatives, business partners or service providers when they share information with us for a legitimate service-related purpose.

· Automatically through website logs, cookies and similar technologies, subject to the choices and controls described in this Policy.

· From publicly available sources or other lawful sources where necessary for a specific service, verification or business purpose.

5. How We Use Information

We may use personal information, as applicable, to:

· Respond to enquiries, provide quotations and communicate about our services.

· Deliver and administer rental management, property-related, project and home services.

· Coordinate property visits, inspections, maintenance, contractors, vendors and other service activities.

· Prepare and administer service agreements, invoices, payments, records and customer support.

· Verify identity, authority, property-related details or information needed to provide a requested service.

· Operate, secure, troubleshoot and improve our website, services and internal processes.

· Send service updates, notices and, where permitted, promotional communications. You may opt out of promotional messages.

· Meet legal, regulatory, accounting and compliance obligations, and establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website may use essential cookies and similar technologies to support website functionality, security and user preferences. We may also use analytics or performance technologies to understand website usage and improve the site, if enabled. The actual cookies, tools, purposes and retention periods must be confirmed by the website administrator and listed here. You can manage cookies through your browser settings; disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality.

7. When We Share Information

We do not intend to sell personal information. We may share information only as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, including with:

· Our group companies or relevant operating entities, where needed to respond to you or provide the requested service.

· Contractors, maintenance teams, brokers, property managers, project partners and other service providers involved in delivering a requested service.

· Technology, hosting, communication, customer-support, analytics and payment service providers engaged to support our operations.

· Professional advisers, auditors, insurers and financial institutions where required for legitimate business purposes.

· Government authorities, regulators, courts or law-enforcement agencies where disclosure is required or permitted by law.

· A successor or transaction counterparty in connection with a merger, restructuring, financing, acquisition or transfer of business, subject to appropriate safeguards.

8. Cross-Border Processing

Some of our technology or service providers may process information on servers located outside India. Where personal information is transferred outside India, we will take steps required by applicable Indian law and any other applicable legal requirements. The locations and providers used should be confirmed before this Policy is published.

9. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards designed to protect personal information against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, loss or misuse. Safeguards may include access controls, authentication, secure hosting, backups and staff or vendor procedures, as appropriate. No website, system or method of transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, including service delivery, record-keeping, dispute resolution, security and compliance with legal obligations. Retention periods vary by the type of information and service. When information is no longer required, we will take steps to delete it or otherwise handle it in accordance with applicable law.

11. Your Choices and Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law and verification of your identity, you may be able to request access to information about the personal data we process, correction or updating of inaccurate information, erasure where legally permissible, withdrawal of consent where processing is based on consent, or raise a grievance about our handling of personal information. Withdrawal of consent does not affect processing carried out before withdrawal and may affect our ability to provide a service that depends on that information. We may retain information where required or permitted by law.

To make a privacy request, use the contact details in Section 14. Please provide enough information for us to identify you and understand your request. We may need to verify your identity or authority before acting on a request. We will respond within the timelines prescribed by applicable law.

12. Children’s Privacy

Our services and website are not intended to solicit personal information directly from children without the involvement or authorisation of a parent or lawful guardian, where such authorisation is required by law. If you believe a child’s personal information has been provided to us improperly, please contact us so that we can review the matter and take appropriate action.

13. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or platforms. Those third parties operate under their own privacy policies and practices. We are not responsible for their content, security or privacy practices. Please review their policies before providing information to them.
